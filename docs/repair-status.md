## Audit provenance and status rules

The earlier 104-item audit was not included in this task and was not found in the repository. Its numbering has not been invented or reconstructed. The IDs below are a fresh baseline audit; reconciliation with the earlier audit is pending until the original audit is supplied.

Allowed statuses are `open`, `implemented-unverified`, `verified-fixed`, `blocked-external`, and `not-applicable`. The original baseline rows are preserved for traceability; implementation evidence and post-repair verification are recorded in `docs/post-repair-tests.md`. Findings that still require deployment, credential rotation, or manual-device/content validation remain open or blocked-external.

Severity meanings: critical = credential compromise or broad security/reliability failure; high = core feature, safety, privacy, or injection failure; medium = material UX/maintainability problem; low = limited polish/documentation issue.
